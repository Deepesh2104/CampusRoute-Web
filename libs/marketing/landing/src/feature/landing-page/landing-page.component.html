    What CampusRoute is not

    • A bank or lender. Lenders alone decide on sanction.
    • A promise of admission, scholarship, visa, placement or salary.
    • A replacement for official college, exam or government portals.
    • A tool to monitor students or family finances.

    Questions families ask

    Who is CampusRoute for first?

    Students and parents making private engineering decisions in India. We will widen based on what real users teach us.

    Are the costs official?

    Each line is labelled official or estimated, with a source and date. An unknown fee is marked unknown, never counted as zero.

    Can I get a loan through CampusRoute?

    We help you compare terms and prepare documents. The lender makes the decision, and you submit only when you confirm.

    Can colleges pay to rank higher?

    No. Sponsored placements are labelled and cannot change a score. Commissions and referral relationships are disclosed.

    Do you cover study abroad?

    International education is part of the long-term vision and planned for a later phase, after the India-first path is validated.

    Is my data safe?

    You choose what is shared, with a preview before anything is sent. You can export or delete your data, and we never sell student or family financial data.

    Will it work in my language?

    Regional languages and low-bandwidth use are core design goals. Which languages launch first is still being decided with early users.

    What will it cost?

    Pricing is still being validated with early users. Join the list and help shape it.
